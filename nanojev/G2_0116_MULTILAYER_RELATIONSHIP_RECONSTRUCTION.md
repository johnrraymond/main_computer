# G2-0116 — Multilayer Relationship Reconstruction Laboratory

Status: implemented experimental protocol; awaits operator execution on installed CUDA checkpoints. No measured 0116 scientific result yet. Research only; G3 remains locked. G2 here means the established G2 binding classes (`owns`, `refers_to`, `measures`, `applies_to`), not G3 relation classes or downstream symbolic reasoning.

## Why

G1-0113 established complete core oracle recoverability on its tested population. G1-0114 converted reader complementarity and agreement into practical extraction. Interim G1-0115 reached perfect required-object recall on 83 fresh variants from six independent problems. With all endpoints present, the historical G2-0087 native classifier still had 1,379 missing and 774 spurious edges. We conditionally accept G1 as sufficient to proceed, rather than require the remaining 2,925 0115 prompts to finish. We do not assume that G2 is solved or that the advanced G2-0103 classifier has the same deficiencies as G2-0087.

The scientific question is whether the backbone's numerical reading surfaces provide complementary relationship competence, and whether a gold-free reconstruction procedure can integrate that competence. Investigate actual upstream and downstream errors rather than training blindly.

## What and how

1. Verify the 0113 cache and trained G1 reader hashes, 0114 evidence/selection, 0115 development selection (completion of 0115 certification is NOT required), 0095/0096 frozen expert registry and checkpoint hashes. No writes outside the research folder. Preserve the exact original 0115 pre-fix seal when applicable.
2. Reuse the 0113 cache. Train on 96 original train prompts with explicitly teacher-forced exact gold endpoints (unalignable cases recorded), plus the observed 0114 endpoints of eight previously exposed test bases (64 prompts, four bases per family). Validate on 64 0113 validation prompts with 0114 predicted endpoints. These are all development populations. No fresh evaluation labels participate. The gold-training arm isolates relationship learning; observed endpoints teach the no-binding class on spurious objects.
3. First score seven frozen constituent G2 heads plus historical 0087 on validation. The seven heads are the actual named 0095/0096 checkpoint lineage used by the later G2 experiments. Verify their non-G2 parameter hash with the existing expert loader. Calibrate their decision thresholds on validation; this is a new comparator, not replay of 0103. Historical native 0087 is also measured at its fixed native threshold on fresh cases.
4. Fit 15 relationship readers on the same cached data, with the same width, training order, loss weighting and epoch budget except the explicitly named intervention arms. Pool each object span's start, mean and end hidden vectors; project through a shared node readout, add kind and coordinate features, contextualize over the current endpoint inventory and classify every ordered pair including NONE. The matched controls are original embedding L0, original/S L7, L14, L21, L28, L14+21, L7+14+21+28. Additional original-all-layer variants: independent per-relation binary losses (specialist), and explicit normalized text equality (lexical control). Geometry is present in all matched arms; embedding-only is not a pure no-context control because the reader itself includes attention. The specialist changes the loss; the lexical arm changes the available evidence. No answer-specific prompts or gold inputs at operational inference.
5. Select reader epoch and decision thresholds using validation only. Compare mean and max probability, unweighted agreement, architecture-group agreement, minority confidence rescue, per-relation weighted mixture and optional train-derived directed edge capacities. The probabilities are model scores, not guaranteed calibrated probabilities. Assembly enforces one positive class per ordered pair and no self edges. Learned capacity constraints are optional, greedy and not a complete semantic graph validity proof. Investigate a greedy reduction frontier from all new readers and frozen experts to smaller inventories; retain noninferior candidates within 0.1 percentage points F1 and one exact graph on development. Choose the operational binding policy by development F1, exact count, then precision. All selections and checkpoints are hashed before fresh generation.
6. Generate a default fresh diagnostic of **12 independent base problems / 48 prompts**, three base problems per generator/family combination. Both allocation and encounter, both 0110 and 0093 generators. 0110 transformations: control, alias+forward, pronoun+forward, caps. 0093: base, role swap, added distractor, action removal. Exclude known prompt hashes including 0113 and the previously sealed 0115 population. Round-robin order: all 12 independent base controls first, then subsequent variants. Distinct seeds and case IDs; not a guarantee of real-world domain diversity.
7. Run blind prediction once per prompt with original and S-conditioned Qwen; reuse those two captured surfaces for every G1/G2 reader. Evaluate original frozen G1, 0114 balanced, and 0115 recall endpoints using the same G2 checkpoint/policies. Reuse predictions if endpoint mappings are identical. Cache hidden states and commit each complete shard atomically. Store individual and integrated predictions and union evidence including supporting readers and probability. No fresh answers are opened by prediction. Verify all prediction shards and seal before scoring.
8. After prediction lock, score against gold and separately perform exact gold-endpoint diagnostic on the cached representations using the frozen readers and policies. If exact token alignment is impossible, report the excluded cases; do not round coordinates and pretend perfect endpoints. This is teacher-forced endpoint analysis, not operational accuracy or selection. Compare it to the same-cohort operational routes, with excluded cases made explicit.
9. Report actual binding precision, recall, F1, exact edge graphs, strict surface graphs (objects and binding edges), reachable and unreachable misses, G1 object metrics, typed edge errors, union recovery, single-reader oracle, gold-filtered union compatibility, raw positive top-rank union, agreement and confidence within agreement, cross-source/layer support, unique contributions, paired author-ID invariance, family/mode/base strata and case-level failures. A raw-positive top-rank union is an optimistic diagnostic, not an operational prediction. Gold-filtered compatibility is not a gold-free selection procedure. Author IDs are only used after blind lock for paired diagnostic, never as inference features.
10. Generate `RESEARCH_RECORD.md` and a concrete bottleneck recommendation. A proposed heuristic (not release criteria): reopen G1 when upstream misses exceed reachable misses, when the recall route improves binding F1 by at least 2 percentage points while reducing upstream misses, or when full gold-endpoint intervention improves F1 by at least 2 points over both routes on the same population. Otherwise compare selected missing edges absent from the reader union against available-but-rejected edges. If reachable misses or extras persist, investigate G2 reader or reconstruction deficiencies. No automatic production promotion or G3 unlock. This heuristic is a research triage decision, subject to inspection of the per-base diagnostics and exact-graph outcomes.

## Explicit baseline limitation

The available repository snapshot contains `nanojev_gamer_v1_g2_admission_stability_0103.py`, an unrelated older experiment. The later `nanojev_gamer_v1_g2_raw_graph_reranking_0103.py` source needed to reuse its true router/reranker inference interface was not available for inspection. Its reported 92.013% F1 and 61/275 exact graphs were measured on reused fit CV; the full-fit candidate was research-only. We must NOT substitute the older admission-stability module, or call the direct constituent-head comparison a replay of the 0103 reranker. The audit reports installed raw-reranker source availability, hash and relevant function signatures without executing an invented API. Integrating the actual raw reranker on the same endpoint surfaces remains the missing baseline step. The core new multilayer experiment can proceed immediately and preserve its cache for that comparison.

## Execution and outputs

Extract this patch into the repository root. Run from `nanojev`:

```powershell
& $py -u -m tools.nanojev_gamer_v1_g2_multilayer_reconstruction_0116 audit
& $py -u -m tools.nanojev_gamer_v1_g2_multilayer_reconstruction_0116 selftest
& $py -u -m tools.nanojev_gamer_v1_g2_multilayer_reconstruction_0116 run --device cuda
```

`run` executes `develop`, `prepare`, `predict`, `score` in separate processes. Repeat the same command to resume completed reader fits and fresh prediction/oracle shards. A configuration change requires a different research output folder (e.g. `--output ../runtime/reports/gamer-v1/g2-multilayer-reconstruction-0116-expansion --bases 24`). The default 12 bases are a deliberately small research probe, not a predeclared certification sample. The reduction search may repeat after an interruption during development; completed neural reader fits are reused. No need to finish the remaining 0115 run. If concurrent 0115 is consuming GPU, suspend it normally before starting this run.

Output root: `runtime/reports/gamer-v1/g2-multilayer-reconstruction-0116/`. `development.json`, `selection.json`, per-reader safetensors and receipts, `fresh/seal.json`, public and separate secret gold files, per-case cache/prediction/oracle shards, prediction lock, `result.json` and `RESEARCH_RECORD.md`. No canonical checkpoint overwritten; only new research reader weights written. All outputs distinguish known observations, provisional conclusions and unresolved tests. CPU selftest exercises actual repository G2 head, all 15 gradient paths, exact coordinate mapping, edge exclusivity, reconstruction and checkpoint fit/resume. Actual installed runtime checkpoint/CUDA and the primary 0110 generator have not been exercised locally.
